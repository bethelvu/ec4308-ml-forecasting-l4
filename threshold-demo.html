Orthonormal design X′X = IP, as on slides 8–10. Ridge: β̂jOLS/(1+λ) (slide 9). LASSO: soft threshold at λ/2 (slide 10). Best subset is written here in penalized form RSS + λ·#{βj≠0}, whose solution keeps β̂jOLS unchanged if |β̂jOLS| > √λ and sets it to 0 otherwise (hard threshold). The example coefficients are illustrative numbers, not data.
